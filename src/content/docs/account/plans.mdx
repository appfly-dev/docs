Check [Plans and usage](https://fly.appfly.dev/account/billing) for your current plan, balance, and checkout prices.

| Plan | Apps | Monthly AI credits | Exports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | 200 | Watermarked, smaller, 100 credits per export |
| Solo | Up to 5 | 5,000 | Full resolution, no watermark, no export credit charge |
| Pro | Unlimited | 10,000 | Full resolution, no watermark, no export credit charge |

New accounts receive 1,000 welcome AI credits once. Monthly credits renew each period. Welcome credits and monthly credits are separate grants.

Solo and Pro include screenshot and listing translations. Pro adds the Appfly AI chat (beta), App Store Connect and Google Play sending and releases (push, then submit for review or roll out, and track), custom product pages, A/B tests, the review inbox, and custom website domains. See the [full comparison](https://appfly.dev/pricing/).

## Common credit costs

| Operation | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| AI screenshot layout or video storyboard | 50 |
| Project translation | 25 |
| Screenshot or website restyle | 10 |
| Rewrite a website section | 15 |
| Write a website page | 10 |
| Standard AI image | 100 |
| Free-plan export | 100 |

Illustrated generation can add image costs. Check the tool description and account balance before starting a larger operation.

Creating a non-AI mockup video project through MCP does not use generation credits. Its export still follows the account’s export rules.
