Appfly helps prepare the assets for a release. Review the text and visuals before you export or send them to another service.

- [Export images and videos](/publishing/exports) to download PNG files, screenshot bundles, and MP4 videos.
- [Connect App Store Connect](/publishing/app-store-connect) to send supported assets and listing text to an editable version, and release it with Pro.
- [Connect Google Play](/publishing/google-play) to send your listing text, screenshots, and feature graphic to the store, and roll out a build you uploaded, with Pro.
- [Publish an app website](/tools/websites) from the website editor.

Pro can submit a release to App Review, or roll out a build on Google Play, from the release flow in the browser. Appfly never submits or rolls out automatically, and its MCP server cannot do either.
