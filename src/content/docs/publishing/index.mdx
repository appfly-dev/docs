Appfly helps prepare the assets for a release. Review the text and visuals before you export or send them to another service.

- [Export images and videos](/publishing/exports) to download PNG files, screenshot bundles, and MP4 videos.
- [Connect App Store Connect](/publishing/app-store-connect) to send supported assets and listing text to an editable version.
- [Prepare Google Play assets](/publishing/google-play) and upload the downloaded files in Play Console yourself.
- [Publish an app website](/tools/websites) from the website editor.

Store submission stays with you. Appfly does not automatically submit your app for review through its MCP server.
