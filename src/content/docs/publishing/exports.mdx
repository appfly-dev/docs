Open the export controls in your project and choose the sizes and languages you need. Screenshot exports are PNG files; video exports are MP4 files. A screenshot export may also include a ZIP bundle.

Sizes are grouped by store: **App Store** and **Google Play** for screenshots, graphics, and promo videos, with social media and ad sizes alongside. Pick the group that matches where the files will go. To send them straight to a store, connect the app instead: see [App Store Connect](/publishing/app-store-connect) and [Google Play](/publishing/google-play).

## Before exporting

Check the selected app, project, device sizes, and languages. Play a video preview and check that text stays readable throughout the scenes.

Free exports are watermarked, smaller, and cost 100 credits per export. Solo and Pro include full-resolution exports without a watermark or per-export credit charge.

## Background exports

Rendering runs in the background. Wait for the export to finish before downloading. If it fails, read the error, check the project, and try again after correcting the problem.

With MCP, `export_project` returns a `renderId`. Poll `get_export` every few seconds until the status is `done` or `failed`.

## Delete an export

Open **Exports** in your app. Use the menu on a finished, failed or canceled export, or right-click it, and choose **Delete export**. Its files and download links are removed and the project stays. An export that is still running has to finish, or be canceled, first.

To delete several, hold **Cmd** (**Ctrl** on Windows) to show a checkbox on each row, pick them, and delete from the bar at the bottom. Exports that are still running cannot be selected. Old exports are also deleted for you after a while.

With MCP, `list_exports` shows the latest exports and `delete_export` removes one.

## Download links

MCP export links work without signing in and expire after one hour. Call `get_export` again to get fresh links. Treat these links as shared access to the exported files.
