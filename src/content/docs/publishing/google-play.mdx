You can use Appfly to prepare images and promo graphics for a Google Play listing. Choose formats that meet the requirements of the Play Console field where you will use them.

## Workflow

1. Create the images or video in Appfly.
2. Review the text and app screens.
3. Export the supported sizes and formats.
4. Download the files.
5. Upload them in Google Play Console and review the listing there.

Appfly’s Google Play workflow is an asset export workflow. It is not a direct connection that uploads or submits your Android app.

Check the current requirements in Play Console before preparing a final release. See [exports](/publishing/exports) for download formats and plan limits.
