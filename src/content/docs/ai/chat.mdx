Appfly AI is a chat inside Appfly. Open it with the round button at the bottom right of any page inside an app. You do not need to connect your own agent: the chat uses the same tools as the [MCP server](/ai/mcp), in your account. The chat is in beta and is part of the Pro plan.

## What you can ask

| Ask for | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A screenshot set | Appfly AI writes headlines, picks a layout, and builds a project from your app's uploaded screens. |
| A promo video | It builds a storyboard from your uploaded screens or a screen recording. |
| Shorter or different text | It changes the text in a project you name, or the one you have open. |
| A different style | It switches a project to another screenshot style. Your text and screens stay. |
| A translation | It adds languages to a project. |
| A look at your account | It lists your apps and projects, the styles you can choose from, and your plan. |
| A website section | It rewrites all the texts of a website section together (Hero, Features, Screenshots, or Call to action) in a style you name: shorter, punchier, benefit-led, or longer. |
| A website look | It picks new colours and a new font for your website. |
| A website page | It writes your privacy, terms, or support page. If the page already has text, it asks before replacing it. |
| Publishing | It publishes your website when you ask it to. |
| A graphic | It makes a graphic from your screenshots, in a style you name. |
| An app icon | It creates an app icon, or asks the AI to design one: new colours, a new mark, or both. |
| Listing text | It reads your store listing, writes or replaces the text of a language, and adds a variant. Nothing is sent to the App Store. |
| Keywords | It tracks a keyword and shows where your app ranks for the ones you track. |
| An export | It starts the export of a project and follows it. A **Download** button appears when it is ready. |

Try asking:

> Make a screenshot set for my app.

> Translate my newest project into German.

> Change the headlines to be shorter.

> Make the hero on my website punchier, then publish it.

If you are on a project's page, say "this project" and Appfly AI uses that one.

## Follow the work

Each reply shows the steps Appfly AI takes in plain words, such as "Looked up your apps" or "Changed the text". Open the line to see each step.

A screenshot set or promo video takes a little while. The chat shows a progress card that says what is happening, such as writing headlines or drawing backgrounds. When it is ready, the card shows an **Open** button that takes you to the new project. If something goes wrong, the card says why.

An app icon, a graphic, listing text and a keyword get a card too: it shows progress while the chat works, then an **Open** button that takes you to the icon, the graphic, the listing or the keywords page. The list you are looking at refreshes on its own, so the new item appears without a reload.

An editor that is open on a project you change through the chat updates to show the new text.

## What it will not do

The chat only works when you ask. It publishes your website only when you tell it to. It cannot upload files or create apps. It cannot delete anything: not projects, exports, icons, listing text or keywords. If you ask it to delete, it tells you to do that in Appfly yourself. It cannot take a website down. It cannot send anything to the App Store or Google Play, submit a release, or roll one out. You do that yourself in [App Store Connect](/publishing/app-store-connect) or [Google Play](/publishing/google-play). Review every result before you use it.

## Credits and plans

Generating screenshots, videos and graphics, designing icons and translating use [AI credits](/account/plans), the same as in the editor. The chat does not ask first. If your balance is too low, it tells you. Your conversation is saved, so reloading the page does not lose it. Use the bin icon to clear it.

To use your own agent instead, see [Connect an AI agent with MCP](/ai/mcp), or [what an agent can do](/ai/mcp-capabilities).
