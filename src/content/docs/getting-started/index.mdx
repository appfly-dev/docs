Appfly is a browser workspace for the assets around your app: store screenshots, promo videos, device mockups, graphics, listing text, and app websites.

You can work in the editor or connect an AI agent through [MCP](/ai/mcp).

## Your first screenshot set

1. [Open Appfly](https://fly.appfly.dev) and sign in.
2. Create an app and add its name and a short description.
3. Upload a few screens from your app.
4. Open **Screenshots**, choose a template, and arrange the screens.
5. Edit the headlines, device frames, and colors.
6. Review the result and [export your files](/publishing/exports).

Start with Free to try the editors with one app. Check [plans and credits](/account/plans) before generating or exporting.
