Use **Graphics** for social images, ads, and other visuals around your app. Use **App Icons** when you want to work on an icon design.

## Make a graphic

Choose the format for the place you will use the image. Add app screens, device frames, text, and background elements. Review the image at its final size before export.

## Work on an icon

Open an icon design and choose a letter, a symbol or an image for the mark, then set the gradient behind it. Keep the icon readable at a small size.

### AI actions

Press the sparkle button in the header of the icon editor for the AI actions:

- **Design with AI** picks a new gradient and mark.
- **New colors** changes only the colours.
- **New mark** changes only the letter or symbol and its size.

A new design is applied to your icon as an ordinary edit, so you can keep adjusting it. Run an action again for another design. Each costs 10 AI credits, and nothing is spent if the AI is unavailable. Your plan limits how many icons you can keep for an app: the Free plan keeps one.

### Delete several icons

Hold **Cmd** (**Ctrl** on Windows) in the icon list to show a checkbox on each design, then click the ones you want. A bar at the bottom lets you delete them all at once. Your current app icon and exported images stay.

## MCP availability

An agent can make a graphic with `create_graphic`, and create, change and delete app icons with `create_icon`, `update_icon`, `design_icon_with_ai` and `delete_icon`. It can edit a graphic's text but not move its layers. See [what an agent can do](/ai/mcp-capabilities) and the [tool reference](/api/tools).
