Open **Websites** inside your app. Add your app details, screens, and download links, then adjust the page layout in the editor.

Each app can have 1 website on Free, up to 5 on Solo, and up to 15 on Pro.

## Prepare the page

Explain what the app does in a short headline and description. Use app screens to show the main features. Add the store links people should use to download it.

Review the privacy and support pages before publishing. If you use AI to draft text, check that it describes your app’s data use and support process correctly.

## Use AI on the website

- **Rewrite a section.** Select a section in the preview (Hero, Features, Screenshots, or Call to action), then use **Rewrite this section with AI** at the top of its panel. Pick Shorter, Punchier, Benefit-led, or Longer. All the texts in the section are rewritten together so they read as one piece. It changes words only, never your layout, icons, or screens. Undo brings the old words back. Costs 15 credits.
- **Restyle the site.** Open **AI actions** in the header and choose **Restyle with AI** for new colours, a new font, or both. Undo brings the old look back. Costs 10 credits.
- **Write one page.** Open Privacy, Terms, or Support under **Pages** and use **Write with AI**. It writes only that page, and asks before replacing text that is already there. Costs 10 credits.

Nothing goes live until you publish. You can also ask [Appfly AI](/ai/chat) in the chat to do all of this, and to publish.

## Publish

Use the publish controls to put the site on an Appfly address. Pro includes custom website domains and removal of the Powered by Appfly credit. Follow the domain instructions shown in the app for your own domain.

Publishing a website is separate from submitting an app to a store. Check the published pages and download links after each release.

The website AI actions are also available through [MCP](/api/tools): `rewrite_site_section`, `restyle_site`, `write_site_page`, and `publish_site`. Creating a website is still a browser-editor feature.
